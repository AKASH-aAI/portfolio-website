My Focus

I'm Akash Chauhan, and my work sits at the intersection of data analysis, business intelligence, and applied machine learning. I enjoy the full process — from raw, messy data to clean, structured datasets and finally to dashboards and models that answer real questions.

My primary specialization is Data Analytics: SQL-based analysis, Excel and Pivot Table workflows, Python EDA, KPI development, and interactive Power BI dashboards. I also work on Data Science and Machine Learning problems, including classification, regression, clustering, and NLP.

What I Work On

I enjoy solving problems through end-to-end workflows — cleaning and validating data, exploring patterns, building dashboards, testing models, and translating findings into business recommendations. Recent projects span supply chain operations, e-commerce customer analytics, conversion funnel behavior, HR attrition, and practical ML apps using Flask and Streamlit.

My Development Approach

I use AI tools as productivity aids for repetitive implementation, debugging, documentation, and prototyping. I focus my own effort on project logic, integration, testing, and delivering working solutions. I aim to be transparent about how I build — combining good engineering habits with modern productivity tooling.
