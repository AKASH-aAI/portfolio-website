Supply Chain Analytics & Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analyst project focused on supply chain operations, inventory management, warehouse performance, stockouts, backorders, and revenue trends.

View on GitHub Dashboard Folder Graph + Insights PDF Pivot Table Report

Problem Statement

Supply chain operations face recurring issues around inventory shortages, product stockouts, backorders, fulfillment gaps, warehouse inefficiencies, and revenue leakage. Without a connected view of warehouse and inventory data, it becomes difficult to identify where the operational gaps are and how they impact revenue.

  • Inventory shortages
  • Product stockouts
  • Backorders
  • Fulfillment gaps
  • Warehouse inefficiencies
  • Revenue leakage

Business Objectives

  • Understand warehouse-level performance and identify inefficiencies
  • Monitor inventory levels and detect stockout risk
  • Analyze backorders and fulfillment gaps
  • Evaluate demand forecast patterns
  • Assess product category performance
  • Track revenue trends and lead times
  • Deliver KPI monitoring and business recommendations

Analytical Workflow

SQL Data Cleaning → Data Validation → Excel Pivot Analysis → Python EDA → Business Insights → Power BI Dashboard

Technologies Used

SQLMySQLPythonPandasNumPy MatplotlibSeabornExcelPivot Tables Power BIGitGitHub

Key Analysis & Deliverables

  • Warehouse performance analysis
  • Inventory level monitoring
  • Demand forecast analysis
  • Stockout and backorder analysis
  • Product category performance
  • Revenue analysis
  • Lead time monitoring
  • Interactive Power BI dashboard
  • KPI monitoring and business recommendations

Business Value

This project demonstrates a complete analyst workflow — from raw transactional data to a decision-ready dashboard. It connects warehouse and inventory performance to revenue outcomes and provides a framework for monitoring stockouts, backorders, and fulfillment gaps through KPIs.

Note: No specific verified numeric findings (e.g., exact stockout rates or revenue figures) were included in the project brief, so none are presented here.
